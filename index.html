Who we are

A global body, grounded locally.

UEMJ ACM carries the mission of the world's largest computing society onto our own campus — turning global standards for learning and innovation into hands-on, local experience.

// Global Body

Association for Computing Machinery

ACM is the premier membership organization for computing professionals, delivering resources that advance computing as a science and a profession. It hosts the industry's leading Digital Library and serves its global members through journals, conferences, workshops and a dedicated Learning Center.

// Our Chapter

ACM Student Chapter, UEM Jaipur

We engage students year-round through flagship hackathons, technical bootcamps and expert-led sessions — building an ecosystem where ideas ship fast and skills compound.

  • AceHack 4.0 — 36-hour flagship hackathon, 5,000+ registrations, 800+ in-person hackers from 50+ cities.
  • HackUEM 4.0 — 12-hour blockchain hackathon powered by Algorand, 350+ participants.
  • Stacks Fullstack Bootcamp — hands-on fullstack learning for 150+ builders.
  • Git & GitHub Workshops — version-control fundamentals for 250+ students.
  • Distinguished Speaker Sessions — talks by Prof. Dr. Tanmoy Chakraborty (IIT Delhi) and Prof. Dr. Sanjay Mishra.

// Host University

UEM Jaipur

Established in 2012, UEM Jaipur is a premier state private university ranked 1st in North India by the Ministry of Education, with a 32-acre innovation-driven campus.

  • Top Placements — highest package ₹72 LPA, average ₹6.5 LPA.
  • AICTE Mentor Institute — recognized for driving innovation and quality education.
  • Specialized Programs in AI, Machine Learning and emerging technologies.
  • Global Alumni working across top companies worldwide.
